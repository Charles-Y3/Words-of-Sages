## How to read this

Issues are grouped into severity tiers. Tiers 1–3 affect the **Chinese source text itself** (the thing a reader would take as "the classic"); tier 4 is translation-only (Chinese is fine, English is wrong); tier 5 is small and mechanical.

- **Tier 1 — Wrong content substituted.** The chapter's `text.zh` is actually a *different* chapter's text, or (once) a quote from an entirely different classical work.
- **Tier 2 — Truncated.** The verse just stops partway through; real content is missing, not altered.
- **Tier 3 — Fabricated/altered ending.** A genuine passage was replaced with invented wording, sometimes borrowing phrases from other chapters.
- **Tier 4 — English mistranslation.** Chinese is authentic and complete; the English rendering is wrong, reversed, or garbled.
- **Tier 5 — Character-level / formatting.** Single wrong characters (mostly from the Simplified→Traditional conversion pass) or stray formatting artifacts.

## Addendum (2026-07-28) — independent re-verification

An independent re-check of all 56 flagged items against canonical Dao De Jing / Great Learning wording confirmed 54 of 56 were genuinely and fully fixed. Two were only partially fixed, and one unrelated typo was found in passing. All three have now been corrected:

| Chapter | What was still wrong | Fix applied |
|---|---|---|
| Tao Te Ching 22 | The original Tier 2 fix restored the missing `text.en`, but `text.zh` was still missing the clause "夫唯不爭，故天下莫能與之爭" (present in English, dropped from Chinese). `plain.zh` also stopped short of covering 不自見/不自是/不自伐/不自矜/夫唯不爭, while `plain.en` already covered all of it. | Inserted the missing clause into `text.zh`; extended `plain.zh` to match `plain.en`'s coverage. `src/data/taoTeChing/taoTeChing3.js`, id 22. |
| Tao Te Ching 24 | The Tier 4 fix corrected `text.en`/`text.zh` to the genuine "餘食贅行" (leftover food/tumorous growths) passage, but `plain.zh`/`plain.en` were never updated and still carried the old, unrelated "those destined to fail are inevitably so" paraphrase. | Rewrote `plain.zh`/`plain.en` to reflect the leftover-food/tumor metaphor. `src/data/taoTeChing/taoTeChing3.js`, id 24. |
| Tao Te Ching 23 | Unrelated typo not caught by the original audit: "熟為此者？天地。" used 熟 (cooked/familiar) instead of 孰 (who). | Corrected 熟→孰 in `text.zh`. `src/data/taoTeChing/taoTeChing3.js`, id 23. |

No other discrepancies were found in Tao Te Ching, and a spot-check of six unflagged chapters (1, 5, 8, 10, 17, 40) found no corruption. Status confirmed RESOLVED for Tao Te Ching as of this addendum.

A separate fine-grained re-check of all 29 Great Learning entries (not just the 10 originally flagged) found the dataset was **not actually complete**, despite appearing to be a full sentence-level breakdown of the classic. Five entire traditional commentary passages were missing outright — the whole 傳一章 (釋明明德), 傳二章 (釋新民), most of 傳三章 (釋止於至善, only its closing poem survived, misplaced after 傳六章), 傳四章 (釋本末), and 傳五章 (釋格物致知, Zhu Xi's supplementary text) — plus the true closing paragraph after the old final entry. These have now been authored and inserted (following Zhu Xi's standard 大學章句 arrangement, sourced from the canonical text), expanding the work from 29 to 37 entries. Additionally found and fixed in the same pass:
- id 11 (old numbering) / now id 19: dropped repeated clause "故君子必慎其獨也" (this refrain occurs twice in the canonical 誠意 chapter; only the first occurrence was present).
- 5:22 (old) / now id 29: mistranslation — "其所令反其所好，而民不從" was rendered as commands contradicting "what people desire" rather than the ruler's own contradictory conduct (a subject-inversion error of the same kind as the previously-fixed 2:11).
- 6:24 (old) / now id 31: two character typos — "興悌" should be "興弟", and "毋以交左" was missing 於 ("毋以交於左").
- 6:27 (old) / now id 34: character-transposition typo — "嫉疾以惡之" should be "媢嫉以惡之".
- 6:28 (old) / now id 35: mistranslation — "唯仁人放流之" was rendered as the benevolent "releasing those with virtue" rather than banishing the wicked/envious minister described in the preceding quotation (another subject-inversion error).
- 5:21 (old) / now id 28: a stray unsupported clause ("sincerity alone, sought wholeheartedly, is enough") had been added to the English translation with no corresponding Chinese text; removed.

`node scripts/validate-data.mjs` confirms all 37 Great Learning entries are structurally sound (sequential ids, no empty fields); `README.md`'s documented section count has been updated from 29 to 37. Status confirmed RESOLVED for Great Learning as of this addendum.
